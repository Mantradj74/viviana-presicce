Pronte a Rinascere · A path of rebirth

A guided pathto be reborn,at every turning point.

Pronte a Rinascere is my path for anyone at a threshold: a birth, a difficult season, the need to change direction. I walk beside you with the knowledge of a midwife and the tools of thirty years of inner practice, until you feel ready to start again.

See the path Talk to me

The path

Four stages, walked together.

Rebirth is not a leap in the dark. It has clear stages, and you never walk them alone: I bring what I know of the body and of the inner life.

TodayYour new beginning

  1. Stop and listen

    We look honestly at where you are, without judgement. Naming the starting point is already the first step.

  2. Understand

    Clear, reliable knowledge about what is happening in your body, your mind and your life.

  3. Practise

    Breath, body and meditation: simple tools you use every day, not only when things get hard.

  4. Begin again

    A new direction, chosen and lived with awareness, and the strength to keep walking it on your own.

Who it is for

Many thresholds, one way to cross them.

MotherhoodBecoming a motherPregnancy, birth and the first months: the deepest rebirth of all, lived with knowledge and calm.Difficult momentsThrough a hard seasonWhen something has broken, or simply worn out. Space to stop, understand and slowly find your footing again.ChangeReady for a turning pointWhen you know it is time to change, improve or take a new direction, and you want someone expert beside you.

Where science meets spirit

Three pillars, one person.

Every change happens at every level of a person at once, and every level deserves care.

Science

Knowledge you can trust

Twenty years of clinical experience as a midwife: verified information, explained clearly.

Body & breath

Tools for the body

Breathing, movement and awareness of the body, to feel safe in what you are living.

Spirit

An inner rebirth

Meditation and inner work to meet fear, find your strength and choose consciously.
