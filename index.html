My story

Thirty years, one thread.

I work at the meeting point of clinical care and inner wisdom. This is how the two grew together.

  1. 01 The beginning

    Meditation and philosophy, before anything else

    My path began long before I became a midwife. I started studying meditation and philosophy thirty years ago, and I have never stopped.

  2. 02 Decades of study

    Learning directly from great masters

    I studied for decades with great spiritual masters, including lamas within the lineage I now teach. Not reading about a tradition: receiving it, directly, over years.

  3. 03 Birth work

    Midwifery, alongside the same study

    In birth I found the same truth meditation had already shown me: transformation is never only physical. It happens at every level of a person, at once.

  4. 04 Bringing it together

    Writing, teaching, leading

    Two books on maternity, the first online platform for maternity courses, a podcast on maternity. Teaching at the meeting point of both worlds.

  5. 05 Today

    President & Senior Instructor, TIIS

    I lead The Institute for Inner Sciences and edit its magazine, The Joyful Soul. Science and spirit, held together in one practice.

Teaching & mentorship

Test it through your own experience.

“I don’t ask anyone to take what I teach on faith. Thirty years of study, practice and direct transmission have taught me one thing above all: test it through your own experience, and trust what you find.”

  • 01

    Attending a workshop?

    My workshops start wherever you are, with no pretense required. Practical, embodied learning, not lectures: you leave with something you can actually feel and use.

    See upcoming workshops
  • 02

    Organizing an event with me?

    I have led workshops and retreats internationally, bringing TIIS teachings (Pranic Energy Healing, Feng Shui and meditation) to centers and communities. I handle content and structure; you focus on your space and your people.

    Discuss hosting
  • 03

    Becoming an instructor?

    As Senior Instructor of TIIS I mentor aspiring teachers from their first steps as Aspirants to certified Instructors. Not a fast certification: real mentorship, over time.
