Birth as a threshold

Why transformation in birth is never only physical, and what thirty years beside women have taught me.

#Birth#Science & spirit

Example article: Viviana will replace it with her own.

Every birth I have attended has shown me the same truth: a woman does not only bring a child into the world. She crosses a threshold, and arrives somewhere new.

The body knows how to do its work. What often needs support is everything around it: the fear, the expectations, the story a woman carries about herself. This is where clinical care and inner work meet.
