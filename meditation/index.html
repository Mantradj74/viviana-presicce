The Institute for Inner Sciences

A lineage, a community.

TIIS brings together students and instructors who study energy work and meditation as inner sciences: practical, testable, rooted in a living tradition.

I lead the Institute as President and Senior Instructor, and edit its magazine, The Joyful Soul.

Becoming an instructor?

As Senior Instructor of TIIS I mentor aspiring teachers from their first steps as Aspirants to certified Instructors. Not a fast certification: real mentorship, over time.

About the instructor path

The instructor path

From student to teacher.

A path walked over time, with personal mentorship at every step.

  1. Aspirant

    You deepen your own practice and start assisting in workshops, with regular guidance.

  2. Training & mentorship

    Structured training in the TIIS teachings, supervised teaching and one-to-one mentorship.

  3. Certified Instructor

    You teach in your own right, as part of an international community of instructors.
