About

Thirty years, one thread.

I work at the meeting point of clinical care and inner wisdom. I don’t choose between science and spirit: I’ve learned both are needed to truly support a person through transformation.

Read my story

My story

One path, not two careers.

Meditation and philosophy came first. Midwifery grew alongside them, not after.

I met birth after years of inner practice, and I recognised it immediately: the same truth meditation had already shown me. Transformation is never only physical. It happens at every level of a person, at once.

For fifteen years I worked in hospital, in the delivery room, prepared for every emergency but always with the woman and her birth at the centre. Then I chose to widen my training, in Italy, England and Austria, and to bring everything together in my own work.

Today I teach, write and lead from the same place: a clinical mind and an inner discipline, working together.

The path

Five chapters, one practice.

  1. 01

    The beginning

    Meditation and philosophy, before anything else

    My path began long before I became a midwife. I started studying meditation and philosophy thirty years ago, and I have never stopped.

  2. 02

    Decades of study

    Learning directly from great masters

    I studied for decades with great spiritual masters, including lamas within the lineage I now teach. Not reading about a tradition: receiving it, directly, over years.

  3. 03

    Birth work

    Midwifery, alongside the same study

    Over twenty years beside women, fifteen of them in the delivery room, trained for every kind of emergency while keeping physiology and the woman at the centre.

  4. 04

    Bringing it together

    Writing, teaching, leading

    Two books on birth and postpartum, the first online platform for maternity courses, a podcast on maternity, and the Pronte a Rinascere method.

  5. 05

    Today

    President & Senior Instructor, TIIS

    I lead The Institute for Inner Sciences and edit its magazine, The Joyful Soul. Science and spirit, held together in one practice.
